## This week

This week you learn to make a podcast, and to interview someone and be interviewed about creative work. Underneath both is one skill: saying out loud what you made, why you made the choices you did, how you used AI, and what you added as a person. You will need that skill in other classes, in oral exams and presentations, and in job interviews. As more written work gets assumed to be AI-made, live conversation is where people find out what you know.

## Three jobs

- **Host:** asks the questions and stays with the guest, not the screen.
- **Guest:** talks about their project, their choices, and their use of AI.
- **Producer:** runs the pre-interview, watches the transcript during the live interview, and passes the host the follow-ups worth asking.
