## The flow

1. **Pre-interview.** The producer interviews the guest, loosely, and records it. The transcript becomes the context: what the project is, what happened, what the guest cares about.
2. **Practice run.** The guest records a short solo version and keeps it private. Hearing yourself once makes the second time better.
3. **Host prep.** The host gives the pre-interview transcript to AI and asks for questions. Then the host cuts the list to the five best and puts them in order. Choosing is your job, not the AI's.
4. **Live interview.** Recorded. The producer watches the transcript and passes the host follow-ups.

## Three kinds of question

- **Choices:** Why this and not that? What did you cut? What did you try first that didn't work?
- **AI:** What did the AI give you that you threw out, and why? How did you know that output wasn't good enough? What would a beginner have typed, and what did you do differently?
- **What's yours:** What are you now expert in? What can you do now that you couldn't in September? What did you add that the AI couldn't?

## Follow-ups

The best question is usually the second one. When an answer is general, ask for the specific moment.

- "Show me the moment that decision happened."
- "What else did you consider?"
- "What would you change with another week?"
- "Say more about that."
- "What do you mean by ___?" (use their word)

## As the guest

- Prepare bullet points, never a script. You want to remember what to say, not recite it.
- Ask AI for the three questions you'd least like to be asked, and think about those.
- Give an example. One concrete moment is better than any general claim about your process.
- It's fine to pause before answering.

## As the producer

- During the pre-interview, be curious and let the guest ramble. You're collecting material.
- During the live interview, keep the thread: notice what the guest mentioned and the host hasn't followed up yet.
- Pass the host one question at a time, on paper or by pointing at the screen. The host shouldn't be reading the laptop.
