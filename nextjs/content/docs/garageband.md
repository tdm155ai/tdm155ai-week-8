## Set up

1. Plug the Focusrite into the Mac and the two microphones into inputs 1 and 2. Turn on **48V** only if the microphones are condensers that need phantom power. Dynamic microphones don't.
2. Open GarageBand and choose **Empty Project**, then an **audio** track using the microphone.
3. In **GarageBand → Settings → Audio/MIDI**, set the **input device** to the Focusrite.
4. Make a second audio track. Set the first track's input to **Input 1** and the second's to **Input 2**. Name them after the people: host and guest.
5. Turn off the **metronome** and **count-in** in the Record menu. Speech has no tempo.

## Record both tracks at once

GarageBand records one track unless you enable more. Choose **Track → Configure Track Header** and turn on **Record Enable Button**, then enable both tracks. Press record and both people get their own track.

## Levels

- Have each person talk the way they will in the interview, and turn up that input's gain on the Focusrite until the ring around the knob shows green with an occasional amber flash. Red means turn it down.
- Sit a fist's width or two from the microphone, and talk slightly past it rather than straight into it, so "p" and "b" sounds don't thump.
- Record 10 seconds of silence at the start ("room tone"). It's useful for editing later.

## Hearing yourself

- **Headphones in the Focusrite:** turn on **Direct Monitor** and leave track monitoring off in GarageBand. There's no delay.
- **Headphones in the Mac:** turn Direct Monitor off and turn on monitoring on the track in GarageBand. There's a slight delay. Don't use Bluetooth headphones for this.
- **No headphones:** fine for today's practice. Watch the levels and listen back afterward.

## Export

**Share → Export Song to Disk**, as MP3 or WAV. That file is your episode. For editing it later, see week 7's audio sheets.

## The machine at the same time

The machine on this site can record from the same Focusrite while GarageBand does. GarageBand makes the good recording, and the machine makes the transcript and suggests questions to the producer.
